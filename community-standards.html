Community Standards

Welcome to Whisperwave! We are dedicated to fostering a safe, respectful, and inclusive online space. Our Community Standards establish guidelines for appropriate behavior and content on our platform, ensuring that all users can engage in a positive and secure environment.

1. Violence and Threats

We do not tolerate threats, glorification of violence, or incitement of harm against individuals or groups. Content promoting physical harm, self-harm, or terrorism is strictly prohibited.

2. Hate Speech and Discrimination

Hate speech targeting race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, gender, or sexual orientation is not allowed. We remove content that promotes discrimination, exclusion, or violence against any group.

3. Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, non-consensual intimate content, and sexual exploitation. Violations will be reported to authorities where necessary.

4. Youth Protection

To safeguard minors, we prohibit any form of child exploitation, abuse, or inappropriate interactions with minors. Whisperwave actively monitors and removes harmful content involving minors.

5. Bullying and Harassment

We do not permit cyberbullying, harassment, stalking, or doxxing. Repeated unwanted contact or efforts to intimidate others may result in account suspension.

6. Human Trafficking and Exploitation

Content promoting human trafficking, forced labor, or exploitation of individuals is banned. Whisperwave collaborates with relevant organizations to combat these threats.

7. Misinformation and Manipulation

We prohibit the spread of harmful misinformation, manipulated media intended to deceive, and coordinated inauthentic behavior that manipulates public discourse.

8. User Safety Measures

We implement safety features, such as age verification, restricted direct messaging for minors, and privacy controls to ensure a secure experience for all users.

9. Enforcement and Reporting

Users who violate our Community Standards may face content removal, account suspension, or reporting to law enforcement. We encourage users to report violations via our in-app reporting system.

10. Continuous Improvement

We are committed to ongoing improvements, staff training, and industry collaboration to ensure our policies remain effective in creating a safe online community.
